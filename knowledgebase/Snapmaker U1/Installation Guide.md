The official assembly and first-print guide. The useful part before you start is the accessory box: Snapmaker ships a surprising number of spares and service tools, and several are easy to miss in the packaging.

## In the printer box

- U1 printer, **4 toolheads**, **4 filament spools**, **4 filament holders**
- **2 filament feeders**, **1 waste collector**
- 4 toolhead cables, 1 AC power cable
- Safety guidelines and the Quick Start Guide

## Tool and accessory box

**Spares and service parts**
- Spare hot end kit ×1
- Heater clamp spring ×1
- Cable holder ×1
- M3 × 12 screws ×2, M2.5 × 16 screws ×9, ST2.6 × 10 screws ×9
- Hex keys H1.5 and H2.0

**Cleaning and finishing**
- Wiping cloth ×1
- Scraper ×1
- Wire brush ×1
- Nozzle cleaning needle ×1
- Nozzle cleaning brush ×4
- Nozzle parking pad ×4
- Thermal grease ×1
- White lithium grease ×1

**Filament and cable routing**
- Diagonal pliers ×1
- Clip ×14
- Filament tube holder ×4
- 4 mm filament tube ×4
- 6 mm filament tube ×5

## Practical notes

- The **diagonal pliers** are for cutting the bent or curled end of the filament before feeding — not for metal.
- Keep the **4 mm** and **6 mm** tubes separate: 4 mm goes to the toolheads, 6 mm runs from the feeder into the machine body.
- The **spare hot end kit** and **heater clamp spring** are the easiest parts to lose. Put them somewhere labelled.
- The **nozzle cleaning needle** and **brush** are for cold/manual nozzle cleaning; don’t poke a hot nozzle.
- The **thermal grease** is for hot-end thermal contact; the **white lithium grease** is for the machine’s motion hardware. Check the guide before applying either.
